Slide Geometry
- Slide size: 1280x720
- Safe margins: 72px left/right, 56px top, 48px bottom
- Grid: 12-column feel with 32px gutters; image-led slides use strong left text zone and right visual zone.

Background System
- Warm paper base for explanatory slides: `#F7F1EA`
- Deep ink base for product screen slide: `#081321`
- Full-bleed image slides use opaque editorial panels instead of translucent dashboard overlays.

Typography
- Claim title: Aptos Display, 46-62px, bold
- Cover/close claim: Aptos Display, 58-64px, bold
- Body: Aptos, 18-23px
- Kicker: Aptos, 11-12px, bold
- Footer/source: Aptos, 10px
- Japanese copy stays short and manually line-broken.

Palette
- Ink: `#1E1915`
- Warm paper: `#F7F1EA`
- Sand: `#DCCDBB`
- Gold accent: `#D8B98B`
- Mist blue: `#B9CAD8`
- Deep navy: `#081321`
- White: `#FFFDF9`
- Usage: warm paper dominates light slides, deep navy reserved for product-screen slide, gold and mist blue used only as accents.

Chart / Diagram Grammar
- No fake charts.
- Workflow and value relationships are shown as cards, rails, chips, and editorial layout blocks.
- Connectors are omitted unless they carry real directionality.

Container / Box Grammar
- Large editorial panels on image-led slides.
- Soft filled rectangles for cards.
- Minimal outlines; prefer fill separation and hairline rules.

Source / Footer Grammar
- Hairline footer rule.
- Small single-line source note aligned left.
- Page number aligned right.

Title / Kicker Grammar
- English uppercase kicker with small square marker.
- Kicker marker and label share a centered y-axis.
- Claim titles are conclusion-led, not topic labels.

Data Label Grammar
- Chips and short labels stay single-line.
- Attribute chips are warm paper or navy pills with strong contrast.

Brand Asset Policy
- No invented logos or mascots.
- Use only product name as text and real app screenshot.
- Generated imagery is atmospheric support only, never factual UI.

Allowed Layout Families
- Cover with full-bleed image + left panel
- Clean problem slide with stacked issue cards
- Workflow slide with text rail + right visual
- Dark product screenshot slide
- Privacy slide with left text zone + right sanctuary visual
- Value slide with top claim + bottom value cards
- Closing slide with full-bleed image + message panel

Banned Motifs
- Generic SaaS dashboard chrome
- Repeated rounded card grids across consecutive slides
- Imagegen text, fake UI, or pseudo-logos
- Decorative connector spaghetti with no semantics
- Overcrowded KPI stacks
